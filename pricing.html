Pricing questions

Is there a paid version?

No. There is one build, and every feature is in it.

Can I use Intercept at work?

Yes. The MIT licence permits commercial use. There is no support contract during the preview.

Do I need an account or a licence key?

No. Intercept has no account, licence key or activation, and the app makes no outbound connections.

Where do I get help?

Read the documentation, or report a problem on GitHub. Report security issues privately, as the security brief describes.
