# Intercept 0.3.0-preview.18

Preview 18 improves saved activity in Simple Alerts:

- Repeated software-input findings now form live groups with recipient counts and plain explanations. Review opens the exact findings in Advanced mode; group Snooze uses one audited action with Undo. Locked guardrails stay outside routine quieting.
- “Quiet the noise” suggestions use saved evidence for a known recipient. Suggestions change nothing until selected, and do not identify the sending program.
- Recognised-agent history now searches and pages across retained history, with full saved counts and 50 events per page. Incoming and backdated activity do not shift the pages you are browsing; Reload includes newer activity.
- Unavailable history shows an unknown count and an explicit reload action. It is not presented as zero activity or a complete list from the latest snapshot.
- Older saved events open by their exact IDs. Recognised activity remains in history, and recipient/time evidence is not promoted into proof of who sent input.
- Undoing an EasyPrivacy conversion now supports long history paths while retaining the supplied source and converted bytes.

This preview retains the reviewed firewall, cleanup, decoy, backend, guard-host and desktop-exit controls from earlier previews. Full V5 and the remaining handover/design audit are still unfinished.

The desktop source remains private. This public repository distributes the website, installers, release notes and checksums. The Windows 11 x64 installer is unsigned; compare its published SHA-256 before installation.

Essential release checks use the optimized paired binaries and packaged app in fresh disposable profiles with collection paused and input hooks disabled. They do not install over your app or change your Windows services, registry, credentials or sign-in setup. Physical input/timing, real-agent calibration, actual installed-service switching, SCM/UAC and sign-in trials, and broad Windows/large-history matrices remain optional and unrun. Missing calibration does not enable a native protection route.

Existing guard storage requirements remain unchanged: features introduced in earlier previews can require storage v8, which older previews cannot reopen. Recorded collection history is retained.
